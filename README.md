# IoT in Smart Buildings: Automation, Efficiency, and Implementation Barriers

**Author:** Youssef Hatem Farid Ahmed  
**Institution:** Faculty of Engineering, Ain Shams University  
**Course:** Scientific Research

## Overview

This project investigates the role of Internet of Things (IoT) technologies in enhancing automation and efficiency in smart building systems, while examining the barriers affecting their implementation.

## Research Question

What is the role of IoT in enhancing automation and efficiency in smart building systems, and what barriers arise during implementation?

## Research Development

The repository documents the development of the literature review through three stages:

1. **Literature Scan:** Searching for, selecting, and evaluating relevant academic papers.
2. **Initial Paper:** The first paper submission, including the subsequent TA feedback and revisions.
3. **Final Paper:** The completed version of the literature review, to be added after final submission.

## Research Scope

- Real-world applications of IoT in smart buildings.
- IoT architectures and Building Management Systems (BMS).
- Data-driven automation and occupancy detection.
- Energy efficiency and building management.
- Implementation barriers, including cost, technical reliability, and privacy.
